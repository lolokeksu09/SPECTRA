# White v21: photo-guided side details

Based on the v20 export and photographs of the white phone, resize/reposition the connected volume rocker to 19.5 mm at Y=36 mm, shorten/reposition the power key to 10.4 mm at Y=13.4 mm, translate the opposite single key +6.5 mm toward the top and translate the two upper side antenna ribbons -4 mm toward the bottom. All numbers are visual approximations from photos, not measured factory CAD. Lower side seam positions and the two bottom USB-C separators (±5.9 mm) remain unchanged. The four side ribbons use a muted neutral gray ceramic material instead of the v20 very light material, while preserving their fitted geometry.

References: https://m.sohu.com/a/1024316068_258768 and https://mobile.zol.com.cn/1183/11838624.html. The latter shows the same side details under different lighting and perspective; both establish approximate proportions, not exact measurements. The rocker stays connected and its divider follows its new centre.

`refine-white-v21.py` extends the existing v9→v15→v17→v18→v19→v20 build. `check-white-v21.py` protects 91 untouched meshes, texture pixels, UVs and PBR values, checks side positions, key topology and dimensions, body dimensions 163.96×77.32×8.29 mm, USB seams, black asset hash, normal validity and export budgets. Khronos validation and Android Filament comparisons run in CI. There are no changes to production rendering, lighting, gestures or packaged source assets. Native Android screenshots compare v20/v21 with the actual production viewer; the API 35 emulator is not physical-device validation. The previous dev.50 release remains the recovery build.
