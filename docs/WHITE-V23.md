# White v23: camera-ring PBR tuning

The v22 emulator captures show clipped white camera rings and visible speckles at normal viewing distance. This revision reduces base-color intensity and metallic response and increases roughness on the camera's machined rings, under the existing SPECTRA lighting. No new texture or light is introduced. The exact result must be assessed on both emulator captures and the physical phone; changes in material values alone do not establish that aliasing has disappeared.

The existing v22 GLB is the input. All 99 meshes retain their exact vertices, faces, UVs and texture pixels; all 87 non-target materials are protected. Left raised optics and near-flush right sensor/flash depth are retained. Camera centres, housing, body dimensions, side details and logos stay in place, as does the black asset. `check-white-v23.py` verifies these conditions, normals and the size budget. The packaging workflow also runs glTF validation, unit tests, APK assembly, and v22/v23 Android Filament captures. Device photos and fixed Filament lighting constrain the visual tuning; the physical Legion Y70 has not been tested. The previous dev.52 release is the recovery version.
